From stems to practice

A simple drum practice workflow.

StemPlayer starts with separated stems. From there, you decide what stays in the backing track and what you play yourself.

01

Load your stems

Open prepared audio stems and keep the instruments you want to hear.

02

Mute the drums

Take the original drum part out of the practice mix so that part is yours.

03

Loop and slow it down

Repeat difficult sections and reduce playback speed while working through fills, transitions, and timing.

04

Export the backing track

When the mix is ready, export a WAV backing track and practice away from the full StemPlayer session.

Built around practice

More than simply muting one track.

Removing the drum stem creates space. The useful part is what you can do next: repeat a difficult passage, change playback speed, adjust the key when needed, balance the remaining stems, and turn the result into a reusable backing track.

A–B Loop Repeat the exact section you need to work on.
Speed Control Slow difficult sections down, then work back toward full speed.
Pitch Shift Change the key when your practice setup calls for it.
Stem Mix + Export Choose the parts that remain and export the resulting backing track.
One important detail

StemPlayer works with separated stems.

StemPlayer starts after stem separation. Bring prepared stems into StemPlayer, then mute, mix, loop, slow down, change pitch, practice, and export your backing track.
